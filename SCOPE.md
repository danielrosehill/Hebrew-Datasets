# Scope: Hebrew Datasets

Part of the [Hebrew AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI) index. This list covers **datasets** that improve support for modern Hebrew across AI tasks.

## In scope

- Hebrew-language datasets: speech and audio, read-speech and TTS voices, sentence datasets, text corpora, instruction and chat data, task datasets (NER, sentiment, QA, nikud), OCR and handwriting, benchmarks.
- English-Hebrew code-switching data and he↔en parallel text.
- Notable multilingual datasets with a usable Hebrew portion (a Hebrew config or split), in a separate *Multilingual datasets with a Hebrew portion* subsection, never mixed with the Hebrew-specific subsections.

The Hebrew-specific/multilingual test is in [docs/classification.md](docs/classification.md).

## Out of scope

- **Israeli AI in general.** Israel is the only country where modern Hebrew is an official language, so it is the natural centre of activity, and many entries here are Israeli. But an AI company, project or person that just happens to be based in Israel is out of scope: that list is far too large, and this set is about Hebrew-language AI specifically. Israel-focused AI is indexed separately in [Israeli-AI](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Israeli-AI).
- Data about Israel that isn't Hebrew-language data (for example open-government catalogues), unless it's usable as Hebrew text or speech.
- Models ([Hebrew-AI-Models](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Models)) and people/organisations ([Hebrew-AI-Community](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Hebrew-AI-Community)).
